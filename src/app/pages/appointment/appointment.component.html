Online appointment booking

Book your dental visit online.

Pick your branch, service, dentist, time, and notes. Signed-in patients have bookings linked to their account automatically using their stored ID number.

Booking flow

Step 1Select branch, service, and dentist
Step 2Choose date, time, and duration
Step 3Receive confirmation
  • Patients can create an account to track and cancel bookings
  • Each booking stores the patient's ID number for easier association
  • Overlapping bookings and out-of-hours bookings are blocked
