Appointment booking

Book your dental appointment.

Search the clinic data in real time, then choose a date and complete your booking.

Booking steps

Step 1:Search for a branch, service, and dentist
Step 2:Choose a date, time, and duration
Step 3:Confirm the appointment details
  • Available time slots load automatically once you pick a dentist and date
  • Signed-in patients can track and cancel their bookings
  • Overlapping bookings and out-of-hours bookings are blocked
